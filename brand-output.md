## Step 5: Brand Persona
Step 5 has been rewritten. The key change: **The Magician replaces The Sage as secondary archetype.** Here's why:

### Selection: Hero (70%) + Magician (30%)

**PRIMARY: THE HERO**
Maps directly to every Winning Zone benefit from Step 4:

| Winning Zone Benefit | Hero Emotion |
|---|---|
| Gasless swapping | Liberation — "I broke free from what trapped me" |
| One-click consolidation | Decisive control — "One action. Done." |
| Smelt-and-earn pipeline | Forward momentum — "I'm earning while others are still figuring it out" |
| Auto-smelt triggers | Preparedness — "My discipline protects me" |
| Speed-first routing | Competitive edge — "I'm out first" |
| Tax-optimized routing | Strategic mastery — "Not just fast, smart" |

**SECONDARY: THE MAGICIAN (replacing The Sage)**

Three reasons for the switch:

1. **The product IS transformation, not information.** Scattered altcoins across 6 chains become productive stables in one click. That's alchemy, not education. The Sage would explain how routing works. The Magician makes the complexity disappear.

2. **"Gasless" is a Magician promise.** It doesn't optimize a broken flow — it makes the broken thing not exist anymore. That's transformation, not wisdom.

3. **The brand metaphor demands it.** "Smelt" is literally alchemy — raw ore into refined metal. The metallurgy identity, the transformation visual, the before/after spectacle — all Magician territory. Layering Sage onto a Magician metaphor creates dissonance.

**Where Sage energy still lives:** Tax optimization copy, analytics dashboards, educational blog posts. These are individual touchpoints that use Sage tone, not the core brand identity.

**Combined essence: "Decisive Transformation"** — A competent trader takes one decisive action (Hero), and the product makes something extraordinary happen (Magician).
